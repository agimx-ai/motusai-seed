# MotusAI Seed 0.3.19

This update improves reading tips and managing submissions.

## Improvements

- **Image preview**: Click an image in a tip to open it, zoom in or out, or download the original. The SDK includes the same preview component.
- **Tips**: The sidebar and tabs show new articles and submission activity. Tip lists mark articles you have liked and update when you return from the detail view.
- **My submissions**: Review progress refreshes automatically. Expand older feedback to read and copy it, or open Obsidian to revise a returned submission.
- **Reading themes**: Removed grid textures from theme backgrounds for easier reading.

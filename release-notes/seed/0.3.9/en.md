# MotusAI Seed 0.3.9

Add clear loading feedback to plugin detail pages while content is being prepared.

## Improvements

- **Detail page loading**: Show a small spinner and “Loading plugin…” when a plugin is first opened or reloaded after an update or development reload. Hide the indicator when content is ready and show an error if loading fails.
- **Interface consistency**: Match the client's typography and theme, support Chinese and English, and respect the system's reduced-motion preference.
